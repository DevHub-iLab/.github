# 👋 Welcome to Innovation Lab @ NTU CCDS

> Bridging the gap between classroom learning and real-world building.

We are a student-led community at the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS) at NTU. Our mission is to take what we learn in the lecture halls and turn it into tangible, real-world impact. We create a supportive, hands-on environment where students can experiment, fail safely, and ultimately grow as builders, mentors, and innovators.

---

## Our Four Pillars

* **DevHub:** Technical interview prep, hands-on workshops, and our annual SummerBuild hackathon.
* **Catalyst:** Senior-junior mentorship programme.
* **TechTalks:** Talks and workshops from industry speakers and peers.
* **Student Entrepreneurship Programme:** Turning student ideas into real ventures.

---

## What's in this Organization?

This GitHub organization is the central hub for our internal code, public-facing platforms, and event infrastructure:
*   **Hackathon & Event Infrastructure:** Landing pages, platforms, and tools powering the hackathons and workshops we host.
*   **Our Digital Home:** The source code powering [ilabccds.com](https://ilabccds.com/).
*   **Internal Tools & Projects:** Scripts and internal resources used to keep the lab running smoothly.

## 🌟 For NTU Students: Come Build With Us

Tired of just doing assignments? If you want a space to build and yap, you're in the right place. We are always looking for passionate coders, designers, and speakers to join our community.
* Build real-world projects from the ground up.
* Talk about something you're passionate about.
* Help organize and run high-energy hackathons & workshops.
* Find mentors or pay it forward by mentoring others in the CCDS community.
